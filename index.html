What is StruxLab?

StruxLab is a small lab where structural engineering meets software. I build browser-based engineering tools, write practical tutorials on real-world structural problems, and share project case studies from the field. Whether you're a student, a junior engineer, or a firm looking for technical insight, there's something here for you.

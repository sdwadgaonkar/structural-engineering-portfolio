Browser-based structural engineering tools — calculators, design aids, and visualization apps.

Tools are released progressively. Free tools are marked as such; advanced tools may require a subscription.

Multi-Span Beam Analyzer

Solve a multi-span beam with mixed loads in seconds. FEM solver (Euler-Bernoulli) — point loads, UDLs, trapezoidal loads, moments, settlement. Outputs SFD, BMD, and deflected shape.

Open tool →

Continuous Beam Calculator

Analyze up to 6-span beams without software. Direct stiffness method — point loads, UDLs, trapezoidal loads, reactions, SFD, BMD, deflected shape.

Open tool →

Load Combination Generator

Get LRFD load combos in one click. Computes factored combinations per ASCE 7-22 or IS 875, with the governing case highlighted automatically.

Open tool →

Section Property Calculator

Get section properties for built-up sections instantly. Computes area, centroid, I, S, and r for rectangular built-up shapes, with a live preview.

Open tool →

Column Interaction Diagram

Check your column against ACI 318 in under a minute. Generates the full P-M interaction diagram with nominal and design envelopes, and plots your (Pu, Mu) demand point.

Open tool →

Bracing Stiffness Visualizer Planned

Side-by-side stiffness comparison of X, V, inverted-V, K, and EBF bracing configurations with animated deflected shapes.

Diaphragm Behavior Tool Planned

Visualize rigid vs. semi-rigid diaphragm load distribution across resisting elements for irregular floor plates.

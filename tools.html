Multi-Span Beam Analyzer

Solve a multi-span beam with mixed loads in seconds. FEM solver (Euler-Bernoulli) — point loads, UDLs, trapezoidal loads, moments, settlement. Outputs SFD, BMD, and deflected shape.

Load Combination Generator

Section Property Calculator

Get section properties for built-up sections instantly. Computes area, centroid, I, S, and r for rectangular built-up shapes, with a live preview.

Column Interaction Diagram

Check your column against ACI 318 in under a minute. Generates the full P-M interaction diagram with nominal and design envelopes, and plots your (Pu, Mu) demand point.

Bracing Stiffness Visualizer

See how much stiffer a chevron is than a single diagonal — animated. Compare X-brace, chevron, single diagonal, and EBF side by side with live deflected shapes and lateral stiffness values.

Diaphragm Behavior Tool

See how much the diaphragm assumption changes your frame forces. Compare rigid, semi-rigid, and flexible load distribution across 3 resisting frames — with torsion, center of rigidity, and eccentricity visualized in plan.
